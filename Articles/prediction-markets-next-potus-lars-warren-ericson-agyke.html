Markets versus Polling

The prediction markets are fairly in line: Around Trump 60% versus Biden 40%. This seems to be an excessive amplification of a small and ambiguous spread of around 1.4% in the national polls, which have a margin of error represented by the Kennedy vote of 9.1%.

Where the Kennedy vote goes in the general election can easily blow out the 1.4% advantage Trump seems to have now. I don't think, in the general, that more than 1% of the Kennedy vote will actually go to Kennedy. Not where the stakes are between two very different (except for their common obsession with golf) ideas of what America should be like.

To make this a GPT article, let's ask some GPT's where the Kennedy vote will go. The prompt will be

Kennedy has a 9.1% popularity rating today. Call these the "Kennedy supporters". What percentage of Kennedy supporter's votes will most likely go to Kennedy, Trump, Biden and Other in the general election? Please also express the percentage of the entire popular vote that this represents.
