What is the Hull White model?

The Hull White model is a mathematical model for pricing interest rate derivatives. It is based on the assumption that the short-term interest rate follows a mean-reverting process with a time-dependent mean and volatility. The model can fit the current term structure of interest rates and calibrate to market prices of caplets and swaptions. The model was developed by John C. Hull and Alan D. White in 1991. If you want to learn more about the model, you can check out this Wikipedia article or this Investopedia article.
