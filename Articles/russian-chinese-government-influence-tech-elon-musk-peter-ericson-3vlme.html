Foreign government influence on American tech leaders presents significant national security concerns, particularly when these individuals control companies with critical U.S. government contracts. The cases of Elon Musk and Peter Thiel exemplify how Russia and China have sought to cultivate relationships with influential American tech billionaires who possess sensitive technologies, access to classified information, and political influence. These connections raise questions about potential conflicts of interest, data security, and the protection of American technological advantages in an era of intensifying great power competition.

Russian Influence on Elon Musk

Direct Communications with Vladimir Putin

According to reports by The Wall Street Journal, Elon Musk has maintained ongoing communication with Russian President Vladimir Putin over the past two years [1]. An individual familiar with the matter confirmed to Associated Press that Musk and Putin have engaged in discussions via phone calls, though specifics regarding the frequency, timing, or content of these conversations were not fully disclosed [1]. These communications have apparently covered a range of topics, including personal matters, business interests, and geopolitical issues, as reported by various current and former officials from the U.S., Europe, and Russia [1].

The relationship between Musk and Putin has drawn intense scrutiny given Musk's prominence as the world's wealthiest individual, his ownership of several companies with substantial U.S. government contracts, and his emergence as a significant political figure in the American conservative movement [1]. One particularly concerning reported exchange involved Putin requesting that Musk refrain from activating the Starlink satellite system over a specific region, characterizing this request as a favor to Chinese President Xi Jinping, with whom Putin has cultivated closer relations [1].

National Security Investigations

The reported communications between Musk and the Russian president have triggered formal calls for investigation from U.S. lawmakers. In November 2024, two Democratic senators, Jeanne Shaheen and Jack Reed, demanded that the Departments of Justice and Defense investigate these conversations to determine whether they constitute a security risk, particularly given SpaceX's extensive contracts with the Pentagon and intelligence community [5]. The senators explicitly identified "serious questions regarding Mr. Musk's reliability as a government contractor and a clearance holder" in their communications to government officials [5].

Beyond direct contact with Putin, the senators also expressed concerns about Musk's reported interactions with Sergei Kiriyenko, Putin's first deputy chief of staff, whom Justice Department prosecutors have accused of using social media platforms, including Musk's X (formerly Twitter), to "covertly spread Russian government propaganda" aimed at influencing U.S. elections [5]. The combination of Musk's high-level security clearance, which grants him access to classified information, and these Russian connections has intensified concerns about potential compromise of sensitive national security information [5].

Chinese Influence on Elon Musk

Economic Leverage Through Tesla

China exerts considerable influence over Elon Musk through his substantial business interests in the country, particularly Tesla's extensive operations there [2]. Tesla's facility in Shanghai exists solely because it received explicit approval from the Chinese Communist Party, and the company enjoys unique advantages not available to other international automobile manufacturers [2]. This business relationship creates significant leverage, as Tesla has become heavily dependent on China for production capabilities, battery manufacturing, and a substantial portion of its income [2].

The precariousness of this arrangement creates a clear vulnerability: should Musk lose favor with Beijing, those privileges could be withdrawn, potentially causing Tesla's market value to plummet [2]. This economic reality creates strong incentives for Musk to maintain positive relations with Chinese government officials and avoid actions that might displease them.

Political Engagement and Public Statements

Musk has acknowledged his alignment with Chinese interests on multiple occasions. In 2023, during a discussion with U.S. lawmakers on Twitter, he described himself as "somewhat pro-China" and stated: "I have vested interests in China, but honestly, I think China is underestimated, and the people there are truly wonderful with a lot of positive energy" [6]. These statements reflect a public positioning that extends beyond typical business diplomacy.

Moreover, Musk's engagement with Chinese officials has ventured into explicitly political territory. After meeting with Chinese Foreign Minister Qin in May 2023, the Chinese Foreign Ministry issued a statement indicating that Musk and Tesla opposed economic decoupling between the United States and China—effectively portraying the American businessman as aligned with Chinese economic policy positions in opposition to some U.S. strategic interests [6]. In April 2025, just months before fully endorsing Donald Trump for president, Musk visited China for a public meeting with Premier Liiang to secure regulatory approvals for Tesla's Full Self-Driving technology in the country [6].

Hidden Chinese Investment in Sensitive Technologies

Beyond Tesla, there are reports that Chinese capital has flowed into Musk's other ventures that have direct military implications. According to reporting cited in The Hill, affluent Chinese investors have stealthily invested tens of millions into SpaceX, Neuralink, and xAI through special financial structures designed to conceal their identities [2]. Three asset managers backed by Chinese capital reportedly confirmed selling \$30 million worth of investments in these companies to unidentified investors over a two-year period, with the actual amount potentially being significantly higher [2].

This situation raises serious concerns because these companies develop technologies with clear national security implications. Under China's economic model, where private sector and state interests are closely intertwined, any substantial Chinese investor is likely influenced by, if not directly controlled by, Beijing [2]. If the Communist Party leadership decides it requires access to specific technologies, it possesses numerous means—both legal and illicit—to secure compliance from firms with Chinese investments [2].

Russian Influence on Peter Thiel

Kremlin Outreach Attempts

Unlike Musk, Peter Thiel appears to have been a target of Russian influence operations rather than an engaged participant in ongoing communications with Russian officials. According to reports, Thiel received invitations for private meetings with Vladimir Putin on two separate occasions, in 2018 and 2022 [3].

The first overture occurred in 2018 at a birthday celebration for German entrepreneur Christian Angermayer in Vienna, where Thiel encountered Daniil Bisslinger, a diplomat connected to the Kremlin's Ministry of Foreign Affairs [3]. During this event, Bisslinger casually invited Thiel to attend the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, promising to arrange a private meeting with Putin [3]. This initial invitation did not result in immediate action from Thiel [3].

More than three years later, in January 2022, Bisslinger renewed the invitation amid rising tensions surrounding Russia's impending invasion of Ukraine [3]. By this time, Thiel had become a confidential FBI informant operating under the code name "Philosopher" [3]. Given the timing, Thiel reportedly grew suspicious that the invitation might be part of a psychological operation designed to downplay the looming conflict [3].

Counterintelligence Implications

The FBI reportedly investigated Bisslinger's claims about his ability to arrange meetings with Putin, recognizing the potential counterintelligence significance of these approaches to Thiel [3]. Frank Figliuzzi, a former FBI assistant director, commented on the significance of these invitations, stating: "Anytime a Russian official is inviting someone to meet with Vladimir Putin, that has potential significance" [3].

Thiel's position as co-founder of Palantir, a company with extensive government contracts for data analysis and intelligence work, makes him a particularly valuable target for Russian intelligence services seeking access to sensitive U.S. government information or influence over American technology policy [3].

Chinese Influence Concerns Raised by Thiel

While the search results contain limited information about direct Chinese influence on Peter Thiel personally, they do reveal his public stance on China's influence operations targeting American technology companies. In 2019, speaking at the National Conservatism Conference, Thiel alleged that Google had "engaged in the seemingly treasonous decision to work with the Chinese military" and urged FBI and CIA investigations into the company [4].

Thiel based this accusation on the assumption that every business operating in China is effectively controlled by the government, arguing that Google's decision to operate there automatically made it a partner of the authoritarian state [4]. Though Google denied these allegations, Thiel's public position indicates his awareness of and concern about Chinese government efforts to gain access to advanced American technologies, particularly in artificial intelligence [4].

National Security Implications

The connections between these tech billionaires and foreign governments raise several significant national security concerns. First, both Musk and Thiel control companies with billions of dollars in critical U.S. government contracts, giving them access to classified information and involvement in sensitive national security infrastructure [1] [5]. Musk's SpaceX, in particular, serves as a vital partner to NASA and various satellite initiatives essential for American defense and intelligence capabilities [1].

Second, these individuals wield enormous influence over technological development in areas with dual-use civilian and military applications, including artificial intelligence, space technology, and data analytics. Foreign influence over their decision-making could potentially compromise American technological advantages or lead to technology transfer that benefits strategic competitors [2] [4].

Third, both men have demonstrated significant political influence. Musk has invested substantial sums into political campaigns and transformed his social media platform X into what critics describe as "a hub for Trump supporters, conspiracy theorists, extremists, and Russian propagandists" [1]. This political dimension introduces additional concerns about foreign influence potentially shaping American political discourse and policy decisions through these intermediaries.

Conclusion

The cases of Elon Musk and Peter Thiel illustrate different dimensions of how Russian and Chinese governments attempt to influence prominent American tech leaders. Musk appears to have established ongoing communications with Russian leadership while facing significant economic leverage from China through his Tesla operations. Thiel, meanwhile, appears to have been targeted by Russian influence operations but maintained a more skeptical stance, even serving as an FBI informant.

These relationships highlight the complex challenges facing the United States in an era where private individuals control technological capabilities once reserved for nation-states, and where those same individuals maintain extensive business interests in countries that are strategic competitors to America. The situation calls for heightened scrutiny, robust counterintelligence measures, and careful consideration of how to safeguard critical technologies and information while maintaining the innovation and economic benefits of global technological engagement.
