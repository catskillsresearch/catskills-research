Summary

We assume a notional of $1B. Our daily trade happens at 9:31AM. We use LLMs and sentiment data to forecast 1 day forward prices based on prior day information. We use this as an insight to outperform the S&P 500 by only timing buy and hold of SPY. For risk management we had a 1% stop loss reset daily. The strategy outperformed SPY by 11% in the 01JAN18-31DEC18 period with a 4.7% return and underperformed SPY by 8.4% in the 14JUN23-25SEP24 period but returned 24.9% anyway. It is a drawdown-averse SPY play.

We use the Tiingo news source for article fragments related to SPY. We use FinBert to assign a weight to negative, neutral and positive sentiment for the prior day to the trade. We use Chronos-T5 to forecast a mean 1D forward price for SPY. We use the default realistic margining Brokerage model in QuantConnect.

We compute indicators 14-day RSI, 20-day EMA and 5-day Momentum Percentage up to the day prior to the trade. We hand the sentiment scores, today’s price, Chronos-T5’s 1 day forward price and the indicators to OpenAI gpt-4o model to get a revised forecast for SPY for tomorrow.

Methodology

Overall design and process flow

The following display shows the overall design and process flow, with choices made:

In coming up with design I considered choices for a variety of design elements:

  • News feed. Choices are Benzinga, Tiingo and AskNews, which is an off-QuantConnect (QC) choice I know about from another competition. I didn’t find any articles in Benzinga on QC. Tiingo gives fragments of articles. I elected to use Tiingo.

  • Sentiment Analyzer. QC provides a bag of words sentiment analysis approach which uses a trailing cumulative sum score based on the prior 100 days of news, and Finbert. I could also have used OpenAI for sentiment analysis. I chose Finbert, applied to the prior day’s news for SPY.

  • Time series forecaster (coarse). Alternatives Ire Chronos-T5 provided on QC and Kalman Filter. I chose Chronos-T5.

  • Observable. Price is intuitive but log return is stationary. There may be problems with Chronos-T5 and Kalman on non-positive quantities. I chose price, with the understanding that Chronos-T5 may do appropriate transformations under the hood either explainably by design or unexplainably as a consequence of training. I don’t retrain or fine tune Chronos-T5. That is a hyperparameter in itself: I chose not to fine-tune.

  • Indicators. Organizers suggested adding indicators as features for training. I don’t train, but provide the indicators to ChatGPT. I use the suggested indicators, RSI, EMA and MOMP. There are many more that could be added, so that is a hyperparameter.

  • Times series forecaster (refine). I use ChatGPT with sentiment, indicators and initial forecast to refine the forecast of next day price.

  • Trader. The trading rule is that if tomorrow’s forecast price is greater than today’s, go long, otherwise flatten.

Transaction cost model
For backtesting we used the DefaultBrokerageModel in QuantConnect. This model is used to simulate the rules and fee structures of a generic brokerage in backtesting and paper trading environments. It supports various features such as handling splits, order updates, and different time-in-force instructions. The model does not apply slippage in backtests or paper trading, and it fills market orders immediately and completely. The fee structure for different asset classes is predefined, and trades settle immediately with a margin account.

Comments
This strategy muted losses in a down year and muted gains in an up year. Both down and up years were profitable. It might be a good strategy for someone who likes SPY but wants less downside risk.
Conclusions
Relevance and novelty
The relevance here is to kick the tires on three kinds of LLM application:
  • Sentiment analysis (FinBert)
  • Univariate statistical time series forecasting (Chronos-T5)
  • “Human judgment” style time series forecasting with indicators (ChatGPT)
Given an estimate of next day’s price we implement a simple long-only buy, hold or flatten strategy with a stop loss downside risk mitigator. There is nothing novel in the strategy. The novelty is in employing the above 3 distinctive LLM applications together to achieve a better forecasting result on tomorrow’s open.
Soundness
The hypothesis here is that the various LLM black boxes will prove better at their jobs than other methods. We look at just 2 sample periods.  So we can't really conclude anything about performance.  This experiment is appropriate just for testing the waters of using LLMs in algos.
