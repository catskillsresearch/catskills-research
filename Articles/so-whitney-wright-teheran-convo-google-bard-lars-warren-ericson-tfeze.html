Taddig, the crispy rice dish popular in Iranian cuisine, can be prepared in various ways, each offering slightly different results. Here are some options:

Basic Stovetop Taddig:

This is the classic method using a pot and lid. You'll need basmati rice, water, oil, salt, and spices like saffron or turmeric (optional). Here's a basic recipe:

  • Wash and soak the rice for at least 30 minutes.

  • Boil the rice in salted water until partially cooked.

  • Drain the rice and spread it evenly in a pot with heated oil.

  • Form a well in the center and add your desired spices (saffron, turmeric, etc.).

  • Cover the pot tightly with a lid wrapped in a clean kitchen towel.

  • Reduce heat to low and cook for at least 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until the rice is cooked through and the bottom forms a crispy golden crust.

Pot with Oven Finishing:

Similar to the stovetop method, but you finish the cooking in the oven for a more even, controlled crust. Follow the same steps as the basic method, but after placing the lid on the pot, transfer it to a preheated oven at 300°F (150°C) for 30-40 minutes.

Rice Cooker Taddig:

If you have a rice cooker, you can utilize its steaming function to achieve a perfect taddig. Combine all ingredients in the rice cooker pot, following your appliance's instructions for cooking white rice. The key is to adjust the water amount slightly less than usual to allow for a crispy bottom.

Additional Tips:

  • Use high-quality basmati rice for the best texture and flavor.

  • Soaking the rice helps remove excess starch and prevents mushiness.

  • Don't peek under the lid while cooking, as this can disrupt the steam and affect the crust formation.

  • Use a non-stick pot or season your pot well to prevent sticking and ensure easy release of the crispy taddig.

Explore Diverse Recipes:
