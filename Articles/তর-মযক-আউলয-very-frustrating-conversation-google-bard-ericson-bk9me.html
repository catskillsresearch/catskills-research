  • Hinders ethical development: Over-anthropomorphization can lead to ethical concerns about how AI is treated and used. If we see AI as "feeling" beings, we may be less likely to hold it accountable for its actions.

The key is to find a balance between using human-like language to make AI more approachable and being transparent about its limitations. Here are some tips:
