When I buy frozen pakora from the grocery it comes with a chilled packet of green sauce. What is it? Does it need to be chilled to stay fresh?

The green sauce that comes with frozen pakora from the grocery store is likely a version of green chutney, which is a popular condiment in Indian cuisine. Here are some key points about this sauce:

Green Chutney

Green chutney is a versatile sauce commonly served with pakoras, samosas, and other Indian snacks and appetizers. The typical ingredients include:

- Fresh coriander (cilantro) leaves

- Mint leaves

- Green chilies

- Ginger

- Lemon juice

- Spices like cumin and salt

Some variations may also include ingredients like yogurt or coconut[3][4].

Chilling and Freshness

The green chutney that comes with frozen pakoras is typically chilled for several reasons:

1. Preservation: Chilling helps maintain freshness and extends the shelf life of the sauce[4].

2. Food Safety: Keeping the sauce chilled helps prevent bacterial growth, especially if it contains yogurt.

3. Flavor: Many people prefer the taste of this sauce when it's cold, as it provides a refreshing contrast to hot, fried pakoras.

4. Texture: Chilling can help maintain the consistency of the sauce.

Storage and Use

- The chilled packet should be kept refrigerated until use[4].

- Once opened, it's best to consume the sauce within 2-3 days for optimal freshness and flavor[4].

- If you don't plan to use it immediately, you can freeze the green chutney in ice cube trays for longer storage[2].

While the commercial version that comes with frozen pakoras is designed for convenience and longer shelf life, homemade versions of this sauce are also popular and can be easily prepared fresh[1][3].

How do I make it?

To make green chutney, follow these steps:

Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh coriander (cilantro) leaves

- 1 cup fresh mint leaves

- 2-3 green chilies

- 1 inch piece of ginger

- 1-2 tablespoons lemon juice

- Salt to taste

- 1 teaspoon roasted cumin powder

- 1-2 tablespoons roasted chana dal or peanuts (optional)

Instructions

1. Rinse and roughly chop the coriander and mint leaves.

2. Add the coriander, mint, green chilies, ginger, lemon juice, salt, cumin powder, and chana dal/peanuts (if using) to a blender jar.

3. Add a few tablespoons of cold water and blend to a smooth paste, scraping down the sides as needed.

4. Taste and adjust seasoning if required by adding more salt, lemon juice or chilies.

5. Transfer to a serving bowl or airtight container.

Tips

- Use cold water or ice cubes while blending to prevent oxidation and maintain the bright green color.

- For a thinner consistency, add more water while blending.

- Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

- You can freeze portions in ice cube trays for longer storage.

This versatile chutney pairs well with snacks, sandwiches, and chaats. The cooling mint and cilantro flavors complement spicy Indian dishes perfectly[1][2].
