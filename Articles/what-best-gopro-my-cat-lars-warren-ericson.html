  1. Size and weight: Cats are small animals, so it's important to choose a lightweight and compact GoPro that won't hinder your cat's movements. The smaller the camera, the better it will sit on your cat's body.
  2. Mounting options: Look for a GoPro that offers versatile mounting options. You'll want a secure and comfortable way to attach the camera to your cat's harness or collar. Consider a camera with a compatible chest mount or a harness specifically designed for pet cameras.
  3. Durability: Cats can be curious and playful, so you'll want a rugged and durable camera that can withstand their activities. Look for a GoPro with a robust build quality and a casing that is water-resistant or waterproof, as cats might encounter various environments.
  4. Battery life: Since you'll be capturing your cat's adventures, it's essential to have a camera with a decent battery life. Look for a GoPro model that offers long battery endurance or one that can be connected to an external power source for extended recording.
