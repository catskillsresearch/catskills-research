7 New Space companies to watch

Created on 2026-04-14 14:20

Published on 2026-04-14 14:44

Here are some smaller "New Space" companies on the horizon, to compare and contrast with the big primes like Lockheed Martin and Boeing Defense, Space & Security and Northrop Grumman:

Intuitive Machines

  • Status: Public (NASDAQ: LUNR)

  • Market Cap: ~$3.8 Billion to $5.3 Billion (experiencing some early 2026 trading volatility)

  • Funding: Publicly capitalized (Went public via SPAC in 2023).

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: Intuitive Machines is a space infrastructure and services pioneer focusing heavily on the lunar economy. Best known for their Nova-C lunar landers, they provide delivery services to the Moon for NASA and commercial clients. They are currently expanding their scope to become a comprehensive "infrastructure-as-a-service" provider for cislunar space, building deep-space communication networks and orbital services.

Firefly Aerospace

  • Status: Public (NASDAQ: FLY) – Completed a massive IPO in August 2025.

  • Market Cap: ~$6.0 Billion

  • Funding: Raised $868 million during its 2025 IPO (prior to that, they had raised nearly $700 million in private markets).

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: Firefly provides end-to-end space transportation. Originally known for its carbon-fiber Alpha launch vehicle, the company has grown into a full-service provider featuring the Elytra orbital transfer vehicles and the Blue Ghost lunar lander. Following their recent public debut and acquisition of SciTec, Firefly has heavily expanded into the national security and defense data sectors.

Michael Sikand said on Xitter:

I love $FLY because its business spans the full orbital stack: roughly 20% launch, 40% spacecraft, and 40% defense software.

Axiom Space

  • Status: Private

  • Market Cap/Valuation: Valued at approximately $2 Billion in a March 2025 down-round (down from a previous $2.6B peak).

  • Funding: Nearing $1 Billion in total venture funding. They recently closed a $350 million mix of equity and debt in February 2026 backed by the Qatar Investment Authority and others.

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: Axiom is building the commercial successor to the ISS. Currently, they operate turnkey private astronaut missions, sending commercial and sovereign-sponsored astronauts to the ISS. By 2027/2028, they plan to attach the first modules of "Axiom Station" to the ISS before ultimately detaching it as a free-flying commercial hub. They also hold a $1.26 billion contract to design next-generation spacesuits for NASA's lunar Artemis missions.

  • Status: Private

  • Market Cap/Valuation: N/A (Valuation undisclosed, but heavily capitalized).

  • Funding: Over $1 Billion invested to date. They just closed a massive

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: Vast is pioneering next-generation, artificial-gravity space stations. Backed by significant internal capital, their goal is to enable long-term human habitation in low-Earth orbit and beyond. They are currently preparing to launch Haven-1 (slated to be the world's first commercial space station) in 2027, while aggressively developing the larger, multi-module Haven-2 to compete as an ISS replacement for the US and its allies.

SpinLaunch

  • Status: Private

  • Market Cap/Valuation: N/A

  • Funding: Roughly $180 million in total funding. They recently closed a $30 million round in August 2025 led by ATW Partners and Kongsberg Defence.

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: SpinLaunch is attempting to bypass the traditional rocket equation using a kinetic energy space launch system. By utilizing a massive ground-based vacuum centrifuge, they spin payloads to hypersonic speeds and "throw" them into the upper atmosphere, slashing the fuel costs required for launch. They are currently using this ultra-low capex launch design to rapidly deploy their own proprietary low-earth orbit broadband satellite constellation, called Meridian Space.

Sierra Space

  • Status: Private

  • Market Cap/Valuation: Valued at $8 Billion following their most recent capital raise.

  • Funding: Over $2.29 Billion in total venture funding. They just closed a massive $550 million Series C round in March 2026, led by LuminArx Capital Management.

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: Sierra Space is a rapidly growing aerospace and defense-tech company focused on end-to-end space transportation and orbital destinations. They are best known for developing the reusable Dream Chaser spaceplane—which is slated for a late-2026 demonstration flight—and their inflatable LIFE space habitats. Recently, the company has successfully pivoted to become a major national security contractor, winning massive multi-hundred-million-dollar awards from the Space Development Agency (SDA) to build missile tracking and warning satellites.

Avalanche Energy

  • Status: Private

  • Market Cap/Valuation: Undisclosed

  • Funding: Approximately $105 million in total funding. They recently closed a $29 million venture round in February 2026 led by RA Capital Management, which matches a $10 million Washington State green grant to scale their commercial FusionWERX test facility.

  • Product / Elevator Pitch: Avalanche Energy is pioneering compact, modular micro-fusion reactors, most notably a desk-sized device called the "Orbitron." Designed for extreme portability and decentralized power generation, the Orbitron uses electrostatic fields to trap fusion ions. For the space sector, Avalanche holds contracts with the Defense Innovation Unit (DIU) and DARPA to optimize these micro-fusion reactors for in-space applications. Their technology aims to provide deep-space vessels with high-efficiency propulsion and continuous, high-output power without the massive weight of traditional chemical fuels or large nuclear fission reactors.
