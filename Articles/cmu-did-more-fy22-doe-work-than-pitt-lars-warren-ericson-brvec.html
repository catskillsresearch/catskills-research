CMU did more FY22 DOE work than Pitt

Created on 2023-10-29 15:31

This US Dept of Energy website gives a breakdown of funding by institution by fiscal year in a series of handy spreadsheets. For FY22, CMU did $6,336,375 worth of work for DOE and Pitt did $4,232,908. There are 7 categories of research. The highest rank in any category for CMU was 29 in nuclear physics. The highest rank for Pitt was 26 in high energy physics. None of the top grantees was an Ivy League school. Here is the big picture:

Leaving CMU and Pitt aside, let's look at the top 10 players in each category for FY22. Of note is that MIT is often in the top 10 and CMU and Pitt are not ranked for advanced scientific computing research, which is kind of interesting.
