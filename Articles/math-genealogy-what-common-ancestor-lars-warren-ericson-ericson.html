P.S. More details about that proof

In his longer writeup on The proof of Fermat's Last Theorem, Nigel Boston notes "an interested reader wanting a simple overview of the proof should consult Gouvea, Ribet, Rubin and Silverberg, or my article. A much more detailed overview of the proof is the one given by Darmon, Diamond, and Taylor, and the Boston conference volume contains much useful elaboration on ideas used in the proof. The Seminaire Bourbaki article by Oesterle and Serre is also very enlightening. Of course, one should not overlook the original proof itself." These are all available online:

  • F. Gouvea. A marvelous proof. American Mathematical Monthly, 101:203–222, 1994.
  • K. Ribet. Galois representations and modular forms. Bulletin of AMS, 32:375–402, 1995.
  • K. Rubin and A. Silverberg. A report on Wiles’ Cambridge lectures. Bulletin of AMS, 31:15–38, 1994.
  • N. Boston. A Taylor-made plug for Wiles’ proof. College Mathematics Journal, 26:100–105, 1995.
  • H. Darmon, F. Diamond, and R. Taylor. Fermat’s last theorem. Elliptic curves, modular forms and Fermat’s last theorem (Hong Kong, 1993):2–140, 1997.
  • G. Cornell, J. Silverman, and G. Stevens. Modular forms and Fermat’s last theorem. Springer-Verlag, 1997.
  •  J. Oesterl´e and J.-P. Serre. Travaux de Wiles (et Taylor, . . .). Ast´erisque, 237:319–355, 1996.
  • A. Wiles. Modular elliptic curves and Fermat’s last theorem. Ann. of Math., 141:443–551, 1995.
  • R. Taylor and A. Wiles. Ring-theoretic properties of certain Hecke algebras. Ann. of Math., 141:553–572, 1995.
