  • Timeline and geometry: LEO platforms have high orbital velocity and limited dwell over a given theater. The time-to-target and pointing constraints don’t match the seconds-long terminal window of a hypersonic dive, even if an interceptor were onboard.

  • Endgame maneuvering: Kinzhals’ terminal jinks and dives invalidate midcourse look-ahead solutions. Space-based shots depend on predictable midcourse tracks; terminal-phase unpredictability collapses the engagement window.

  • Logistics and survivability: Weaponizing LEO with interceptors introduces massive cost, legal/political complications, and vulnerability to counterspace actions—none of which align with a near-term, theater-specific need.
