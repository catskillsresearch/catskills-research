Various articles noted that IK-3 was also known as the "Artic Wolf Penal Colony 3". Wolf...or Owl? Finally, Wikipedia, notably less censored (though every word hotly debated in the crowdsourced editing process), gave me both the exact coordinates and the correct mascot and even a reference to the prison's website, which helpfully provides the exact address (629420, Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug,  urban district of Labytnangi, town. Kharp , st. Gagarina 1 A). Wikipedia gives the link to IK-18 which then gives a link to IK-3 which has the same address:

Federal Governmental Institution — penal colony No. 18 of the Federal Penitentiary Service of Russia in Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug, commonly known as the Polar Owl (Russian: Полярная сова, Polyarnaya sova) is a Russian prison located on the bank of the Sob River near the Polar Urals in the Kharp urban-type settlement.
